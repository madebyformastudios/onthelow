# Voice-over Founder Mode, Part 2 BUILD "The Build"

Alleen de gesproken tekst, per beat. Eerste regel van elke beat is het beat-id (voor timing en Remotion).

## Per beat

**hook**

You just signed with an agency. Thirty thousand dollars, six weeks. Most of you voted for it. Problem is, building feels like progress, and nobody has paid you a cent.

**burn**

Fifteen thousand down, fifteen on delivery. Add rent and software, and you're burning twenty-two thousand two hundred a month. Runway, sixty-three days.

**vest**

And somebody is already watching. The Vest has twelve engineers, and his team ships in twenty-eight days. Your agency needs forty-two.

**m1**

Mistake one: say yes to every idea. Each extra feature is a change order, a hundred and fifty dollars an hour.

**m2**

Mistake two: disappear. Agencies build what's written down, and nobody wrote down your customers.

**m3**

Mistake three: wait for perfect. Perfect takes eleven weeks.

**name**

Here's what you don't know. That first mistake has a name. Scope creep. A fixed price locks the cost of their plan, not yours.

**rule**

Rule two: build the smallest thing that can take a payment.

**fix**

So you cut the app down to one feature and a checkout button. The agency says yes to everything. Not one question about your customers. That should worry you.

**counter**

Meanwhile, The Vest opened a free beta. By morning, thirty-one people have left your waitlist.

**cliff**

Week five. Your app works. Mostly. It crashes when two people log in at once, and The Vest just went live. Launch it broken, or pay for two more weeks?

**end**

Comment LAUNCH or WAIT. Part three follows what you pick.
