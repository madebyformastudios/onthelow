# Voice-over Founder Mode, Part 2 SELL "The Ask"

Alleen de gesproken tekst, per beat. Eerste regel van elke beat is het beat-id (voor timing en Remotion).

## Per beat

**hook**

You just put a forty-nine dollar price on an app that doesn't exist. Most of you voted for it. Problem is, strangers don't lie to be nice.

**burn**

First, you tell the developer to stop. Burn drops from fifteen thousand a month to twenty-two hundred. Runway, six hundred and thirty-eight days.

**vest**

But cash isn't your problem now. Time is. The Vest's team ships in twenty-eight days.

**o1**

Option one: post it everywhere. Four hundred likes. Zero dollars. Likes cost nothing to give.

**o2**

Option two: ask your friends again. You already know how that ends.

**o3**

Option three: ask strangers for money. That's the scary one.

**rule**

Rule two: a yes only counts if it comes with a credit card.

**fix**

So you build a one-page site. Forty-nine dollars, fully refundable. You email a hundred strangers who have the problem.

**results**

Eleven reply. Three pay. A hundred and forty-seven dollars, the first money this company has ever made.

**spy**

Here's what you don't know. Customer three is j.carter. A work email, from the company that raised two million dollars. Competitors buy your product to copy it. That has a name. Competitive intelligence.

**counter**

Meanwhile, The Vest's team reads your pricing page. By Friday, they have a free plan.

**cliff**

Day nine, eleven forty-seven p.m. An email. We'll pay twenty-five thousand for your idea and your waitlist. It's from The Vest. Take the cash, or keep going?

**end**

Comment TAKE or KEEP. Part three follows what you pick.
